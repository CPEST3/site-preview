PM SIM

Project Manager for Simulation — activated March 2026 by combining PM SE and PM CT2. Develops synthetic environments, modeling, and readiness tools enabling Soldiers to rehearse convergence and operate in contested, multi-domain environments using AI, 3D gaming engines, and edge devices.

PdM CRT
Product Manager Cyber Readiness Training

Manages the Persistent Cyber Training Environment (PCTE), a platform supporting USCYBERCOM that enables Joint Cyberspace Operations Forces to train at individual, team, and force levels. PCTE provides standardized sustainment training, team certification, and mission rehearsal with realistic, variable conditions. It is one of five elements of the Joint Cyber Warfighting Architecture (JCWA).

CTIA
Common Training Instrumentation Architecture

Provides a flexible product line architectural environment that standardizes reusable, interoperable software components and services for Army live training instrumentation systems. CTIA is the software architecture within the Live Training Transformation (LT2) product line, fielded at combat training centers, home stations, and over 200 live training ranges worldwide — achieving more than $1 billion in cost avoidance over the past decade.
